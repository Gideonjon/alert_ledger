From inbox to answers in three steps

Nothing to connect, nothing to set up. If your bank sends alerts, it works.

  1. Copy your alerts

    Long-press a bank SMS and copy it. Grab as many as you like, from any bank or fintech app.

  2. Paste them in

    Alert Ledger reads the amount, date, bank and who you paid, and sorts each one into a category.

  3. Ask what you want to know

    Totals for a month, every transfer to one person, how often you buy suya. Every answer shows the transactions behind it.

Questions you can finally answer

Ask the way you'd ask a friend. Names, merchants and vague words all work, because the search understands meaning, not just exact text.

"Show transfers to Emeka"

Finds TRF TO EMEKA OKAFOR from Access and NIP/MONIEPOINT/EMEKA OKAFOR from Zenith, even though the alerts look nothing alike.

"How much did I spend on light this month?"

Knows that a PHED PREPAID TOKEN is electricity, and adds up every one.

"How many times did I buy suya?"

Matches OBONG SUYA SPOT EWET without you ever tagging it.

"How much did I receive last month?"

Salary, transfers from friends, client payments. All money in, in one number.

Your money, your eyes only

No bank login, ever
You choose which alerts to paste. Alert Ledger never asks for your bank password, BVN or card.
Only you can see your transactions
Every saved transaction is locked to your account. Searches only ever look at your own data.
Totals you can check
Amounts are added up exactly from your alerts, never estimated. Tap any answer to see the original alert.
It learns your merchants
Fix a category once, and similar alerts land in the right place from then on.
